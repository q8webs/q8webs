# Q8WEBS — cinematic studio redesign

The reference recording was used as visual inspiration: a full-screen illustrated opening, depth while scrolling, and an interactive project showcase. The result keeps the Kuwait studio context, Arabic/English support, existing project artwork, and contact destinations.

## Implementation
- Full-bleed Kuwait night scene with scroll-linked depth and reduced-motion support.
- Three-project gallery with navigation and expandable project information.
- Light services chapter, interactive studio globe, four-stage process, and service-specific WhatsApp messages.
- Saved language preference now restores both content and document direction.
- Globe animation pauses offscreen, in hidden tabs, and for reduced-motion users.
- Existing unverified performance percentages and certification badges are no longer displayed.
- Existing in-progress source was copied into `.design-review/before/` locally before editing. Review files and temporary video tooling are Git-ignored.

## Generated asset
- Tool: built-in ImageGen, not CLI.

A dreamlike futuristic Kuwait waterfront at blue hour: recognizable Kuwait Towers placed on LEFT third, elegant architectural masses, translucent glass monoliths and polished obsidian terraces emerging from calm dark reflective water, subtle cyan light paths across water leading to towers, distant city skyline softened by atmospheric haze. Large glowing pale cyan moon behind towers on left, lavender blue twilight at horizon, almost black navy sky. Sophisticated high-end digital art, architectural visualization, restrained ethereal atmosphere and depth, beautifully rendered reflections. Main scene concentrated on left and lower third, RIGHT half dark quiet negative space for large Arabic headline overlay. Full bleed, no borders. Palette ink navy #060b16, icy cyan #9ce9ef, muted periwinkle. Not a UI screenshot. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Save final image as asset for website.
