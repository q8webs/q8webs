## Final prompts
### Hero
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a cinematic editorial website hero artwork for a premium web and mobile software studio. Wide landscape 16:9. Charcoal black sculptural environment with an irregular jagged black ink and torn carbon-paper aesthetic, subtle vermilion red edge lighting, floating angular graphite fragments. Main subject concentrated on LEFT TWO THIRDS: a large beautifully designed dark desktop monitor at dynamic three-quarter angle, a thin black laptop, and a floating black smartphone, all composed as a dramatic towering sculptural group on black rock. Screens show elegant ivory and brick-red abstract website layout blocks, an abstract mobile dashboard and visual design grids, no readable text. A sweeping thin vermilion light ribbon curves around the devices. RIGHT THIRD intentionally nearly empty very dark charcoal for Arabic heading overlay. Sophisticated art direction, deep blacks, restrained red accents, tactile stone and brushed metal, photorealistic product materials with expressive surreal editorial composition. Hero should feel like premium dark fantasy poster composition but exclusively DIGITAL PRODUCT DEVICES, no characters, no creatures, no weapons, no city, no moon. No text, no logos, no watermarks. No frames or outer white margins. Horizontal 1792x1024 or closest wide size.
### Web
Use case: product-mockup. A standalone transparent-background 3D editorial art asset for a web design studio. One beautifully rendered dark graphite ultra-thin laptop seen in dramatic three-quarter view, opening toward the viewer, with two floating glass browser UI panels behind it and angular obsidian stone fragments below. The laptop screen shows a refined ivory website design with grid layouts, small warm gray typography-like lines and restrained brick-red accents, no readable words. Sweeping vermilion satin ribbon behind the laptop, expressive wisps of black ink and scattered tiny graphite shards around the silhouette. Contemporary high-end product rendering mixed with tactile editorial collage. Diagonal rising composition, generous transparent breathing room around entire silhouette; complete laptop uncut. Charcoal, ivory and deep burnt red only. Strong directional soft light; crisp sculptural metallic detail. Landscape 4:3. No people, no characters, no logo, no watermark, no background scene. Actual transparent alpha background. Keep black rock base narrow and irregular, no rectangle.
### Mobile
Use case: product-mockup. Create a standalone editorial 3D cutout on a truly transparent alpha background, portrait 4:5 composition. Two premium black titanium smartphones suspended diagonally, one forward-facing dominant device leaning slightly left, one behind seen at a side angle. Beautiful front screen with an ivory mobile business app interface: a circular brick-red chart, compact cards, minimal navigation icons, tiny gray typographic placeholder lines only, no readable words, no brand. Sculptural sweeping vermilion red satin ribbon wraps behind and below phones; the phones float above fragmented black obsidian stone with black ink wisps and small debris. Dramatic elegant cinematic studio light, realistic metal and glass, dark charcoal and deep brick red, ivory display, slightly warm paper-like aesthetic. Dynamic advertising art, visually related to an editorial laptop collage. Entire device silhouettes visible, no cropped corners. No humans, no mascots, no game characters, no text, no watermark. No rectangular background or floor. Keep ample transparent negative space around full object.
